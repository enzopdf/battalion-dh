DH Blog

Small reminders for long missions.

01 · BACKLOG

Don't wait for the perfect day

A backlog becomes manageable when you convert it into small, visible tasks and keep moving even after an imperfect day.

02 · ROUTINE

Make the next action obvious

Instead of planning an entire month every night, decide what lecture, questions or revision block comes next.

03 · CONSISTENCY

Protect your sleep

A routine is easier to maintain when sleep is treated as part of the study plan rather than time stolen from it.
